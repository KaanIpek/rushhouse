Rushhouse Diner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026
The short version. Rushhouse Diner is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for your data to sit on. Your progress is saved on your phone and nothing else. The one exception is a rewarded video ad — and only when you tap to watch one. There are no banner ads, no interstitials, and no ad that plays by itself.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer operates no servers, no analytics, and no crash-reporting service, and receives no data from your device. We could not identify a single player if we tried.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps one save file locally, under the key rushhouse-unity-save. It holds:

  • your restaurant layout, day number, cash and star ratings
  • perks and upgrades you have unlocked
  • wardrobe tokens, plus which outfits you own and have equipped
  • your settings (music, sound effects, controls)

This never leaves your phone. Deleting the app erases it permanently — we cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.

Rewarded ads, and only rewarded ads

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob. They appear only where you choose to tap a "watch a video" button — for a second chance at a failed day, bonus cash, or wardrobe tokens. You can play the entire game and never watch one.

When you do choose to watch, Google — not us — receives information from your device in order to serve and measure that ad. Google describes this in its own Privacy & Terms, and it typically includes:

DataWhyWho receives it
Device advertising identifier and device information Serving the ad, capping how often it repeats, detecting fraud Google
IP address Delivering the video, and choosing an ad for the right country Google
Ad interaction (that it was shown, watched, or closed) Measuring the ad and paying the developer Google

None of this is linked to a name, email, or account, because the game has none to link it to.

No tracking prompt on iOS

Rushhouse Diner does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) for that purpose. You will never see the "Allow app to track your activity?" dialog, because the app never requests it. On iOS this means the ads you see are non-personalised.

Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the first launch shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform, asking whether ad partners may use your data. Your answer is honoured — decline and you get non-personalised ads, or none at all.

You can change your mind at any time: open Settings inside the game and choose Privacy options. The same form reopens.

Children

Rushhouse Diner is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and — since we collect nothing from anyone — there is nothing for a parent to request the deletion of. If you have a concern, write to us at the address below.

Your rights

Rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA — access, correction, deletion, and opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information — apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. For the data Google processes when you watch an ad, use Google My Ad Center and your device's ad settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and → Apple Advertising
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID

To erase everything the game itself stores, delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that affects what leaves your device will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
