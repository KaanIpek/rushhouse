Common questions

My progress disappeared.

The game saves only on your own device, with no cloud backup. Deleting and reinstalling the app starts a fresh restaurant, and there is no way to recover the old save — not even for us, because we never receive a copy of it.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Every ad in the game is a rewarded video behind a button you choose to tap — a second chance at a failed day, bonus cash, or wardrobe tokens. Nothing plays on its own, and the whole game can be finished without watching a single one.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland: open Settings in the game and choose Privacy options. The consent form reopens and your new answer replaces the old one.

The kitchen staff aren't cooking.

Plates are assembled by the prepper, so a kitchen with cooks but no prepper will stall with food ready and nothing going out. Hire at least one of each, and make sure the counter is reachable — staff walk around furniture, so a fully boxed-in station blocks them.

A dish burned before I could grab it.

Stoves keep cooking after the dish is ready. Watch for the sizzle marker changing colour, and unlock the timer perk if burning keeps catching you out.
